# Competition coverage expansion

The ESPN fixture collector previously monitored 31 football competitions plus NBA, NFL and NHL. Its reviewed source catalog now monitors all 220 football competition identities exposed by ESPN's core soccer catalog on 1 November 2026, plus 16 team-sport feeds across basketball, American football, ice hockey and baseball. The existing organizer and shared-feed collectors remain enabled for the other sports.

Copa del Rey, Supercopa de España, FA Cup, Community Shield, Coppa Italia, Supercoppa Italiana, DFB-Pokal, German Supercup, Coupe de France, Trophée des Champions, UEFA Super Cup, women's competitions and qualifying rounds have their own source identities. Domestic league rights do not transfer to their cups. The 120-day horizon remains bounded; uncertain kickoff times, undrawn opponents and postponed/cancelled fixtures are withheld from confirmed upcoming listings.

The expanded source run accepted 17,878 future fixtures in 120 competitions, with no failed ESPN slices and no missing competition artwork. A combined local snapshot contained 18,685 events across 145 competition names, including historical records. These are bounded observations, not proof of exhaustive worldwide event or broadcast coverage. Fixtures outside the rolling window or unpublished by sources cannot be asserted as confirmed events.

ESPN college basketball requires group 50; group 80 silently returns no games. College football uses the observed wider group 90. Group selections are part of the cache identity. Daily scoreboards prevent large monthly responses from exceeding the shared response cap. An explicit publisher day whitelist may skip empty days only inside its declared calendar range; dates outside that range are still fetched. Overflowing responses fail explicitly rather than silently truncating a competition.

The competition directory includes reviewed branded profiles between editions, rather than hiding a competition when it has no current fixtures. Basketball national cups and supercups, rugby competitions, ATP/WTA tours and ICC tournaments are included. A profile without fixtures is labelled as awaiting published fixtures and remains excluded from indexing until it has actual events. Alternate names for ACB, LNB and domestic rugby leagues share one directory entry.

Artwork lives under `public/artwork`, with exact source URLs, identity scope and file hashes in `data/competition-artwork-provenance.json`. Qualifiers and relegation playoffs can use their parent competition badge; NCAA categories use the NCAA organizer mark where a distinct tournament badge does not exist. Non-FIFA friendlies use source-publisher category artwork. Copa de la Reina and Copa Bolivia use SVG viewports of the original organizer-published artwork, preserving the original pixels and source hashes. The active Austrian Erste Liga gap is also resolved.

`competition-coverage.json` is written after every collection. It distinguishes missing artwork, failed source slices, published fixtures and competitions awaiting published fixtures. Run `npx tsx scripts/audit-competitions.ts --strict` with `DATA_DIR` pointed at the snapshot to check active artwork and monitored source health.

The larger calendar required indexing fixture reconciliation by source identity and 15-minute sport/time buckets. This preserves the existing participant, competition, drift and ambiguous-match checks while avoiding a full-season scan for every event. Same-ID reschedules remain eligible across dates and cannot inherit old channel allocations.

Validation: 159 regression tests passed before release preparation, including day-whitelist boundaries, cup identities, unannounced kickoffs, artwork hashes, territory isolation and fixture-merge boundaries. Desktop/mobile directory checks decoded every competition image at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels; no horizontal overflow or script errors were observed. Final production counts are recorded separately after deployment and collection.

The first production refresh exposed an integration limit: official schedule enrichment passed the entire enlarged calendar into the external feed validator, which rejects more than 5,000 rows. The handoff now resolves candidates against the complete calendar, validates only actual assignments in bounded slices, and preserves source clocks and ambiguity checks. The external validator limit remains unchanged. Regression coverage includes 6,002 fixtures and ambiguous matches separated by more than 5,000 rows; all 161 tests pass.

Production release `20261101-sports-v46` completed its collection at 23:07:34 UTC on 1 November (2 November in Europe/Paris). The snapshot contains 19,270 events with unique public paths, 122 active competitions, 15 Copa del Rey fixtures and two Spanish Supercup semifinals. The competition coverage report has no missing active competition artwork or failed monitored fixture feeds. Total event counts include retained historical records.

Public browser verification decoded all 256 directory logos at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels, checked the cup/supercup profiles and a new Copa del Rey event page, and found no page errors or horizontal overflow. Every competition represented in the public snapshot, including historical records, has artwork. Eight viewing-country schedule requests returned successfully in 0.59–0.89 seconds on the host. After warming those country views, the web container used 286.4 MiB of its 512 MiB limit. All 161 regression tests passed inside the production image. The previous release remains available for rollback.
